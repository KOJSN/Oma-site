Privacy

What Oma collects, why, and — just as importantly — what it does not.

Last updated 10 September 2026

The short version. Oma is a marketplace for nail appointments in Nigeria. To book one you give us an email address; to be paid as a nail tech you give us more. Your hand photographs are measured on your own phone and are never uploaded — not to us, not to anyone. We do not sell anything about you to anybody, and there is no advertising in Oma.

Who we are

Oma is operated by Oma Nail Services, registered in Nigeria with the Corporate Affairs Commission (RC [RC NUMBER]). We are the data controller for everything described here.

Contact us about anything on this page at hello@omanails.com.

Your hand photographs

They never leave your phone. When you scan your hands, the measurement happens inside the web page on your own device. The image is not uploaded, not stored on our servers, and not seen by us or by any nail tech.

What is saved is the result — a nail shape, a few measurements, and the date — and that is saved on your phone, not ours. Clearing it is one tap in Settings.

If we ever want to keep photographs — to improve how the measurement works — we will ask you first, in the app, in plain words, and you will be able to say no and keep using Oma exactly as before. We will not start doing it quietly and update this page afterwards.

Identity checks for nail techs

A nail tech cannot appear in search until her identity is verified. This is what lets a customer hand money to someone she has never met.

We ask for a virtual NIN (vNIN) — the temporary 16-character token you generate yourself with *346# — and not your actual NIN. The vNIN is passed to a NIMC-licensed verification provider and is never stored by Oma.

What we keep is the answer: whether the check passed, the provider's reference number, and whether the name matched. Not your date of birth, not your address, not your photograph, none of the other information the provider returns.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we never will.
  • There is no advertising in Oma, and no advertising trackers.
  • We do not upload your hand photographs.
  • We do not store card numbers or bank details ourselves.
  • We do not store your NIN.
  • We do not show a customer's full name publicly. On the O points leaderboard, customers appear under a shortened name.

Who else sees it

Only where the service cannot work otherwise:

  • The other person in your booking. Your nail tech sees your name and the appointment; you see hers.
  • Paystack — payments and payouts. They are licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria and have their own privacy policy.
  • Supabase — the database and servers Oma runs on.
  • Our identity provider — the vNIN only, at the moment a nail tech is verified.
  • Our email provider — to deliver your sign-in code.
  • The authorities, if the law requires it of us.

How long we keep it

Your account details for as long as you have an account. Records of completed appointments and payments for longer, because they are financial records and both people in a booking have a claim on them.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my Oma account. It is not hidden behind an email to support.

Your name, email, phone, area and listing are erased, your sign-in is destroyed, and you cannot get back in afterwards. Past appointments remain as financial records with your name removed — an appointment has two people in it, and deleting yours entirely would delete a nail tech's record of work she was paid for.

Oma will refuse to delete your account while money is in the middle — an appointment paid for but not finished, a dispute open, or a balance in your wallet. It tells you exactly what to finish first.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@omanails.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Age

You must be 16 or older to hold an Oma account, whether as a customer or as a nail tech.

Nigerian law treats anyone under 18 as a child. So if you are 16 or 17, you need your parent or guardian's permission to use Oma, and they may contact us about your information at any time using the address below.

We do not knowingly hold anything about anyone under 16. If you believe we do, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes to this page

If we change what we collect, we will change this page and say so in the app before the change takes effect — not afterwards.
