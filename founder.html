Oma was founded and built to make booking a nail tech in Nigeria something you can do without knowing them already.

A note from the founder

Finding a good nail tech in Nigeria usually depends on who you already know. I built Oma so that you can see who is near you, what they charge and what other women thought of them, and so that your money is held until the appointment actually happens.

Nail techs are paid for the work they do. Clients get a fair way to choose. That is what Oma is for.
