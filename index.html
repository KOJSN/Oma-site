How it works

Four steps, and nobody has to trust a stranger.

The hard part of booking a nail tech you have never met is not finding her. It is knowing the money is safe and that she will turn up.

1

Scan your hands

Oma measures your nail beds from a photo, on your phone, and tells you which shapes actually suit them. The photo never leaves the device.

2

Find someone near you

See who is close, what they charge, and what other women said. Or let her come to you — the price for that is shown before you book.

3

Book and pay

You pay into an account for that one appointment. She cannot touch it yet, and neither can we.

4

Show your code

When your nails are done, she scans the code on your phone. That is what releases the money — and it is the only thing that does.

1

List yourself

Your name, your area, your own prices and how long each service takes. Shop or no shop — if you travel, Oma shows where you are while you work.

2

Pass the ID check

One check, so a customer paying a stranger knows who she is paying. Your ID number is never stored — only the fact that it passed.

3

Take bookings

Requests arrive in the app with the services, the time, and whether she is coming to you or you to her.

4

Scan, and get paid

Scan her code when you finish. The money moves to your balance, with every deduction set out line by line.

Paying safely

Your money doesn't move until your nails are done.

Paying a stranger up front is the reason most people never book online. Oma's answer is not a promise — it is where the money physically sits.

If she never scans your code, the appointment never completed, and the money goes back to you.

About us

Oma is for the first appointment.

Finding a good nail tech in Nigeria happens by word of mouth, and word of mouth stops at the edge of who you already know. If you move to a new area, or your usual tech stops answering, you are back to guessing from photos and hoping.

Real prices, before you book

Every tech sets her own. You see the number, and what it would cost for her to come to you, before you commit to anything.

Distance you can trust

Oma asks your phone where you are each time you look, so "1.2 km" is from where you are standing, not where you were last week.

Reviews that had to be earned

Only someone who paid for and completed an appointment can leave one. A tech cannot rate herself and a rival cannot rate her down.

For nail techs

Listing yourself is free. You are paid when you scan.

No monthly fee and nothing to pay to be found. Oma takes a small amount from each completed service — and nothing at all if an appointment is refunded, because then it never happened.

Your prices are yours

Set what you charge for every service, and a different price for going to a customer if you want one.

Nothing taken from your travel

If you travel to a client, the call-out and the transport are yours. Oma's fee is on your service prices only.

No shop? Still findable

Turn on Working when you start your day and customers see where you are. Turn it off and you are off the map.
